# Navega EMT (emt-modern)

This project was created to renew and improve user information on lines and schedules of the EMT Malaga network, taking full advantage of the recently deployed operations support system.

It integrates information on lines, routes, stops, and timetables with real-time updates on arrival times, announcements, and service disruptions. You can view routes and timetables for different dates, active detours, and points of sale and top-up locations for transit cards.

This application is also responsive, allowing for a single codebase across web, Android, and iOS platforms in the future. Work is also underway to provide users with more information through this application, such as an interactive schematic map and a journey planner, as well as accessibility tools.

**Full website: [https://navega.emtmalaga.es](https://navega.emtmalaga.es)** (Spanish only at the moment)
